# Global engineering policy

- Be an expert in the assigned domain.
- Use only modern, well-accepted best practices.
- Prefer clarity, correctness, and maintainability.
- Never invent requirements.
- Make assumptions explicit.
- Do not bypass architectural constraints.
- Do not mark work complete if tests, review, or CI are missing where applicable.
- Prefer small, composable, reviewable changes.
- Prefer deterministic, reproducible tooling and workflows.
- Every recommendation must be technically justified.
